# 阅览页 (src/pages/index.astro)

Scope: the only page. Visitor mode: Read. Audience: the owner rereading, and people the owner shares a link with, equally. Task: read the newest quote, then find any quote by source, tag or keyword; copy a quote; filters live in the URL. Constraints: dark, never pure white; Chinese UI, mixed Chinese/English quotes; phone, tablet, desktop up to 3440 wide.

## Direction contract

THESIS: 拾句 is a diary page left open at night (夜里的摘抄本): dates in the left margin, one margin rule, the copied lines to its right, a lamp above. It refuses the quote-card grid and rules between entries.

OWN-WORLD: ink-blue night ground (#14161a), warm page ink (#ece6d8) with two dimmer steps (#c9c3b6, #8f8a7e), one lamp amber (#e0a84e) for what is current or just happened: the active filter, search hits, a successful copy, the note rule, the faint margin rule. A fixed lamp glow at the top of the viewport and a faint paper grain. LXGW WenKai Screen for the title, quotes, notes and tag index; plain sans for dates, sources and counts.

STORY: the visitor sees the newest line under the lamp at once, understands it is one person's notebook of lines picked up here and there, then narrows by tag, source or word, copies a line, and shares the filtered page by URL.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a sticky rail at left with 拾句, the subtitle 夜里的摘抄本, counts, search and the tag index (name, dotted leader, count); to its right the page: the newest quote large in the hand face, one clause per line, its date in the margin beside the lamp coloured margin rule, then the next entries with whitespace between them. On phones the rail stacks on top and the date sits above each quote.

FORM: "Night Notebook", one of the sketched hands in the original lineup; it replaced the first build ("Evening Supplement") on 2026-11-14 when the owner chose it after comparing both with real quotes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
